§ 03  THE INVARIANTS a violation is a build failure — never a warning, never a TODO
I1
No unsupported sentence.
Every emitted sentence carries a citation and passes entailment against the spans it cites. Unentailed → repair twice → drop → quarantine.
I2
Numbers are computed, never generated.
Every numeric literal traces to a span, a cited source, or a sandboxed SymPy result. The model phrases; it never originates a value.
I3
Provenance is total.
Every node reachable from a document traces to file or url, locator, retrieval time and content hash.
I4
The prerequisite graph is acyclic.
Cycles break by a deterministic, logged policy — lowest confidence first, tie-broken on content hash. Never silently.
I5
Contract before emission.
Five slots: definition, intuition, worked example, visual-or-analogy, and two assessment items. A concept enters a document only with its status attached.
I6
Contradiction is surfaced, never resolved.
When retrieved evidence disagrees with the source, the source stays primary and the divergence becomes a marked note carrying both citations.
I7
Licence-clean media only.
An image is a bbox crop of the source, generated diagram code, or nothing at all.
These are not aspirations in a design doc. They are the thing the adversarial suite exists to break — and a gate that never rejects anything is broken, not perfect.
§ 04  GROUNDING hover a claim — its source lights in the margin
T he critic never sees the dossier that produced the sentence it is grading. It sees one sentence and, for each, only that sentence's own cited spans — looked up fresh by id. A chapter's concepts merge at cosine 0.801 , every MCQ distractor cites a real misconception node2 , and twelve simulated students screen each item3 before it is allowed near a question paper.
MARGINALIA
1
Tuned against a committed labelled fixture: “Ohm's law”/“V = IR” embed at 0.855; the closest false positives, “variance”/“covariance” at 0.764, sit below the line.
2
Sourced from sentences the critic actually marked contradicted — real material, never invented. A distractor that can't cite one is dropped.
3
Labelled screening, never calibration — twelve students is nowhere near enough for a calibration claim, and the wording is not allowed to drift.
§ 05  WHAT COMES OFF THE PRESS
Booklet
Every contract slot, Mermaid visuals rendered at build time, source figures cropped by stored bbox, and a bibliography walked from the citation edges.
Cheat sheet
A pure template over each concept's own source span — so it makes zero model calls, asserted on the cache-miss counter.
Question paper
Greedy selection against a blueprint of marks, Bloom mix and unit weightage. An infeasible blueprint names its binding constraint.
Answer key
Mirrors the paper's selection and reads each numeric item's stored execution result rather than recomputing anything.
A build with any error-severity diagnostic produces no PDF at all.
The certificate still gets written either way — it is the artifact that explains why.
